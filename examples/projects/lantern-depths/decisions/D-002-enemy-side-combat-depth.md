# D-002: Build combat depth through enemy behavior; keep one-button combat

## Decision

Address combat monotony with Option A from the G-002 escalation: telegraphed enemy attacks, new
enemy behaviors that demand dashing and flanking, and rooms that each ask a different question.
D-001 stays in force. A hold-to-charge attack on the same button may be proposed in a future Goal;
it is out of scope for G-002.

Owner's words: 「Aで。ワンボタンは守りたい。ただ、同じボタンの長押しで溜め攻撃、みたいな案なら将来は
検討してもいい。今回はやらない。」

## Rationale

The playtest traced monotony mainly to enemies that never demand positioning or timing; Option A
targets that directly, keeps the pillars, and is reversible. The Owner wants to protect one-button
combat.

## Alternatives considered

- Option B, a heavy attack on a second button: answers the "heavy hit" remark directly but changes
  D-001; evidence was a single remark.
- Option C, room re-mixing only: low ceiling; folded into A by the Game Director.

## Revisit when

A re-playtest after Option A still attributes monotony to the player's limited actions. Then the
first candidate is a hold-to-charge attack on the same button, per the Owner.
