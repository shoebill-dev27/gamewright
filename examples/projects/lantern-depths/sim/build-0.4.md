# Simulated build 0.4 — what playing it looks like

Dry-run stand-in for a real build: a player-observable description only. No implementation details.

- Room 1: Grubs, as before. Fine as a warm-up.
- Room 2: Spitters now glow orange before they fire. You learn to keep moving between swipes.
- Room 3: a new enemy rushes in a straight line after glowing orange. The first time, it hits you.
  The second time you dash sideways at the glow and get a free hit while it recovers. Corner
  camping no longer works: it lines you up for the rush.
- Room 4: a shielded enemy blocks swipes from the front with a spark. You circle behind it, and
  start choosing which enemy to deal with first. Hitting it during its glow does not stop the bash —
  after one hit you learn to dodge instead.
- Room 5: everything together. Tense and busy. In the darkest corner near the exit, the shielded
  enemy's orange glow is hard to see against the blue-black wall; you get bashed twice without
  noticing the warning.
- The dash is now used constantly in rooms 3–5, mostly to avoid attacks.
- Late in the run you still sometimes wish a hit felt heavier, but less than before.
