# 07: studio → game-designer — Rule on frame-rate tolerance at protection-expiry boundaries (criterion 6)

## Task

Decide what "same results at 30 and 60 FPS" means for the moment protection ends. Specifically, is a difference of up to one frame at a protection-expiry boundary acceptable, and if so, with what tolerance? If it is not acceptable, state the intended behavior. ## Context

Goal G-001 is a defect: the Owner saw the player take damage partway through a dash. `design/combat.md` says the player is invulnerable for the full dash duration. The fix is built (build 0.4). QA verified it from the criteria and the build text. Criteria 1–5 pass: no damage during dashes after a hit, at any delay in the 0.8 s post-hit window, and no regressions to the undashed hit, the dash with no recent hit, or dash distance, duration and cooldown. ## Findings

- The only frame-rate-dependent difference in build 0.4 is the edge-timing statement. Exact wording of `sim/build-0.4.md`:

- Protection ends late, never early. It therefore cannot cause damage inside the 0.25 s dash, and criteria 1 and 2 are unaffected.
- The affected case is a contact that lands within one frame after a protection end time. That covers the dash end and the 0.8 s post-hit expiry, for both Grub and Spitter.
