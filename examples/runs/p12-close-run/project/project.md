# Project: Lantern Depths (sample, playable)

## Vision

A short-session top-down action roguelite: descend through lantern-lit caves, one room at a time.
Pillars:

1. **One-button combat.** Depth comes from positioning, timing and enemy behavior, not from input
   complexity. Attack and dash are the whole kit (D-001).
2. **Short runs.** A full run takes 15–20 minutes.
3. **Readable threats.** Every threat is visible before it can hurt the player.

## Player profile

Action players who want short, intense runs. Comfortable with dodging and spacing; not interested in
memorizing combo lists. Mostly play with a gamepad.

## Current state

Vertical-slice prototype, build 0.3 in `repo/` (sha e86d75dc8d0e): 5 rooms in one cave biome, 2
enemy types, player kit of attack and dash. A play session is an input script (`manual.md`):
`run_play` shows what a player sees, `run_trace` the simulation state, and `run_tests` runs the
build's tests (10 pass).

Fixed (G-001): the dash is now invulnerable for its full duration even when started late in the
post-hit window; verified at 30 and 60 FPS.

Known gap: `design/combat.md` does not say whether the frame on which the dash timer reaches 0 is
still protected. A hit in contact on that frame currently lands. Proposed follow-up Goal for
`game-designer`.

## Constraints

- Desktop, keyboard and gamepad.
- Must play correctly at both 30 and 60 FPS.
- Small team: prefer changes that reuse existing systems.
- No new input buttons beyond attack, dash and pause without Owner approval (D-001).

## Art direction

Dark caves lit by the player's lantern; warm light against cold blue-black. Threats use saturated
orange/red so they read against the dark.

## Design index

- `design/combat.md` — player kit, damage and invulnerability rules, enemies.
- `manual.md` — controls and the play script format (player-facing).
