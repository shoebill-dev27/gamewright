# G-001: Damage taken while dashing

## Owner request

> ダッシュ中なのにダメージを受けることがある。再現できるので直してほしい。

## Interpretation

The Owner reports a defect: the player sometimes takes damage while dashing. The Owner says it is reproducible but gave no steps.

Success condition: in the situations where the Owner sees it, the player no longer takes damage during a dash, to whatever extent the design documents (`design/combat.md` invulnerability rules) say a dash protects the player. It must hold at both 30 and 60 FPS.

Out of scope:
- Changing the dash's design (duration, cooldown, distance, whether it is invulnerable) unless the design documents turn out not to define the case. That would be a Design question, not part of this fix.
- New input buttons (D-001).
- Other combat tuning.

## Unknowns

- Observe: settled. QA reproduced the defect at 30 and 60 FPS (a dash started with under 0.25 s of post-hit invulnerability left; the flag drops mid-dash and a shot hurts).
- Diagnose: skipped. Root cause is the engineer's call.
- Decide: skipped. No choice of direction, priority or scope is open.
- Design: skipped. `design/combat.md` says the player is invulnerable for the full dash, so the intended behavior is defined. Becomes a step again only if the engineer finds the combination of the two invulnerability sources undefined for criterion 3 (invulnerable until 0.8 s after the hit, even after the dash ends).
- Plan (`technical-director`, tentative): needed only if the fix is non-local or FPS-dependent, or if the same criterion fails verification twice.
- Build (`gameplay-engineer`, open): fix and regression test per the defect report.
- Verify (`qa`, planned): always after Build, at 30 and 60 FPS, with scripts A and B.
- Play and Review: skipped. This is a defect, not an experience Goal.
