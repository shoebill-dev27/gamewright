My reasoning so far: at 30 FPS, `hurt_left` is 0.1 s when the dash starts, and the dash lasts 8 frames. The test adds a zero-velocity shot on the player before each frame that is still dash-active, and it never removes them. On the final frame (t=0.9667) the dash ends and the flag clears before `_update_shots` runs, so the shot left over from the previous frame hits. The skip in the test only stopped a new shot being added on that frame. The failure is therefore in the test setup, not in the fix. I'm keeping the assertions and clearing the shots before each frame.
