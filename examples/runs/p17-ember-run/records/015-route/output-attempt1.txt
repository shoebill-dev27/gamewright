The project is a short turn-based roguelike, played by descending a cave. The player profile (in the Load list) describes who it is for. A person would play it in a browser. You play through the text interface the manual describes.

- How the light and time trade-off felt.
- Pacing and rough length of a run, and how different the runs felt.
