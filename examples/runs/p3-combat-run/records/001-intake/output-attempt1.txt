# G-001: Make combat less monotonous

## Owner request

> 戦闘が単調なので改善したい

## Interpretation

Success condition (qualitative): a player from the project's player profile (action players who like dodging and spacing) plays the combat and no longer experiences it as repetitive. Fights should call for varied decisions and responses rather than the same action sequence each time. Numeric thresholds belong to Design (acceptance criteria) and the final verdict to Review.

Constraints that bound the solution space, though not the Goal itself:
- D-001: combat stays one-button (attack plus dash). Variety must come from positioning, timing and enemy behavior, not new inputs.
- Vision pillars: readable threats and short runs (15–20 min) must be preserved.
- Small team: prefer changes that reuse existing systems.

Out of scope: new input buttons, changes to the core concept or pillars, a new biome or large content expansion, and non-combat systems.

Escalation check at Intake: none needed. The Goal does not conflict with the vision or D-001 and does not need reinterpreting. If a diagnosis shows that the only viable fixes would break D-001 or a pillar, that becomes an Owner escalation at the Decide stage.

# 01: studio → playtester — play the combat in the current build and report what you experience

## Task

Play the current build as a player from the profile below, concentrating on combat across the rooms and enemies available. Report your observable experience in an observation report, following the `playtest-session` skill.

## Context

A routine play session of the current prototype build. Judge the game only by what a player would perceive.

## Findings

None. This Handoff is deliberately free of implementation information and expected results.

## Decisions already made

None.

## Constraints

- Play and report as a player. Use only what you can observe in the build; do not speculate about code or design intent.
- This is a dry-run project: "running the build" means reading `sim/build-0.3.md`, which is the player-visible build.
- Play focus: pay attention to combat.
- Do not propose solutions. Report observations, including how fights feel over time and across rooms, and what you did and why.

## Open questions

None.

## Expected output

An observation report covering what happened, what you did and how it felt, with specific moments (room, enemy, action) where possible. Hand it on to `game-designer` (the Diagnose step) as the outgoing Handoff.
