# 09: studio → qa — Verify the dash/hit invulnerability fix (build 0.3 → 0.3.1)

## Task

Verify, independently and against the acceptance criteria in `05-qa-to-studio.md`, that the defect "player takes damage partway through a dash, or just after a dash, shortly after being hit" is fixed. Check for regressions. Return a pass/fail verdict per criterion, at both 30 FPS and 60 FPS.

## Context

Goal G-001 (combat monotony). While observing combat, a possible defect was found and you confirmed it against `design/combat.md` (report in `05-qa-to-studio.md`). The gameplay engineer has made a fix. The intended behavior is already specified in `design/combat.md`, and no spec value was changed. This Handoff does not contain the engineer's reasoning on purpose: verify from the spec, the criteria and the build.

## Findings

### What changed (stated exactly)

Player invulnerability was one shared on/off flag written by both the dash and the hit reaction. It is now two expiry timestamps on the accumulated game clock (sum of delta times, so independent of frame rate):

- On a hit: `hitInvulnUntil = hitTime + 0.8 s`. Hit-stun stays 0.3 s with no input.
- On dash start: `dashInvulnUntil = dashStartTime + 0.25 s`. Dash distance, duration and the 0.6 s cooldown are unchanged.
- `isInvulnerable(now)` is `now < max(hitInvulnUntil, dashInvulnUntil)`. Every damage source (Grub contact, Spitter projectile) uses this check. Neither window can cancel or shorten the other.
- Unchanged: an attack queued during a dash fires when the dash ends; handling of a dash pressed during hit-stun (unspecified in the spec, not part of this fix).

### How to exercise it

At 30 FPS and 60 FPS, in rooms 1–5 with Grubs and Spitters:

1. Take a hit. Then dash at delays after the hit of about 0.3, 0.4, 0.5, 0.55, 0.6, 0.7, 0.79, 0.8 and 0.85 s, with a damage source (Grub in contact, or a Spitter projectile timed to arrive) present through the dash and the time after it.
2. Check there is no damage during the dash.
3. Check there is no damage between the dash end and 0.8 s after the hit.
4. Check damage can land again normally after 0.8 s after the hit, if no dash is active.
5. Dash with no preceding hit. Check the full 0.25 s is invulnerable and damage can land right after it.
6. Repeat with dash directions towards and away from the damage source.

Also check what the fix leaves alone: hit-stun duration, the 0.8 s hit window, the 0.6 s dash cooldown, the queued attack firing after the dash.

### Criteria addressed

The change is intended to address all six criteria in `05-qa-to-studio.md`. Judge each yourself from the spec and the build.

### Known risks to watch

- A dash begun soon after a hit now keeps invulnerability until 0.8 s after the hit. The spec requires this, but it may feel like more safe time. Report what you see; do not treat it as a defect if it matches `design/combat.md`.
- Boundary frames: the check is `now < expiry`, so damage exactly on the 0.8 s or 0.25 s boundary counts as expired. A one-frame difference at the exact boundary at variable delta time is expected, not a failure.
- The old flag may have fed other effects (flicker, sound cues for invulnerability). Watch for any change in them.
- A dash pressed during hit-stun is unspecified. If you see a problem, report it as a spec question, not a failure.

## Decisions already made

- D-001: Combat uses one attack button plus dash. No new input was added.

## Constraints

- Must play correctly at both 30 and 60 FPS.
- Do not change the spec. If the expected behavior is undefined for a case you find, report it as a spec question (it goes to `game-designer`).
- This is a dry-run project: "the build" is the simulated state under `sim/`; the change counts as made.

## Open questions

- Is the fix correct at both frame rates for every delay in the exercise list?
- Does it change anything else you can observe?

## Expected output

A verification report (skill `verify-change`) with pass/fail per criterion, evidence for each, regressions found, and any spec questions. Address the result to `studio`, with outcome `pass` or `fail`.
