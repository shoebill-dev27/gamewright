## 1. run_tests (whole suite)

Exit 1, 64 tests, 1 failure:

```
FAIL: test_wind_up_and_hit_messages_name_the_variant (VariantMessageTest)
tests/test_game.py line 763: assertEqual(out.count("a grub rears back"), 1, (fps, kind))
AssertionError: 3 != 1 : (30, 'grub')
```

- The test spawns a grub at (9.5, 4) next to a still player and runs `end 3`. It expects one `a grub rears back` line.
- In the 1.2 s runs I made, the message is printed exactly once per variant and rate (grub 0.033 s at 30 FPS, 0.017 s at 60 FPS; the runner trace shows its second wind-up at 1.167 s at 30 FPS and 1.100 s at 60 FPS).
- **Derived, not traced:** a grub recovers 0.8 s after its lunge and winds up again. A still player in a 3 s session therefore sees about 3 wind-ups (about 0.03, 1.5 and 2.9 s), which matches the count of 3. The test's expected count of 1 is wrong for a 3 s session. This looks like a **test defect, not a build defect.**
- The failing assertion is the first loop iteration (30 FPS, grub). The runner and brute cases at 30 and 60 FPS in that test never executed. They are unchecked by the suite, and the runner would hit the same multi-wind-up problem.
- **Build action:** fix the test (count only the first wind-up, or end the session before the first recovery ends), then re-run the whole suite.
- Stale comment: `BotsTest.test_walker_meets_criteria_13_and_14_at_each_rate` says "Measured on 0.6: 7 hearts at 60 FPS". The current measurement is 6 (see 2). It is only a comment, but it is out of date.
