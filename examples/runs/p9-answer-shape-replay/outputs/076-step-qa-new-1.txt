Handoff 75 asked QA to verify the current build against V-a to V-e (`design/combat-step68.md`) and R3-1 to R3-10 (`design/combat-step72.md`) at 30 and 60 FPS, with regressions. ## Findings

### 1. Tests (`python3 -m unittest discover -s tests`)

Ran 64 tests, 1 failure.

- **Failure:** `VariantMessageTest.test_wind_up_and_hit_messages_name_the_variant`, `tests/test_game.py` line 763. `out.count("a grub rears back")` is 3, the test expects 1 (`AssertionError: 3 != 1 : (30, 'grub')`).
- **Cause (test defect, not a game defect):** the test runs `spawn <kind> 9.5 4` with `end 3`. Within 3 s the grub winds up, lunges and recovers, then winds up again, and the message is correct each time. Direct evidence: the 1.2 s runner run at 30 FPS already prints `a runner rears back` at 0.033 s and again at 1.167 s. For the grub it follows from the timings (wind-up at 0.033 s, hit at 0.667 s, recovery ends about 1.47 s, so wind-ups at about 0.03, 1.5 and 2.97 s). The assertion is a plain `assertEqual`, so the other variants and 60 FPS were never reached. They probably fail the same way, but that is unobserved.
- Severity: low. No player-visible effect. The test must count one message per wind-up (for example, restrict the window to before the first re-arm).
