### 1. `run_tests` (whole suite)

`python3 -m unittest discover -s tests`: exit 1, 64 tests, 1 failure.

```
FAIL: test_wind_up_and_hit_messages_name_the_variant (test_game.VariantMessageTest)
  line 763: self.assertEqual(out.count(f"a {kind} rears back"), 1, (fps, kind))
  AssertionError: 3 != 1 : (30, 'grub')
```

- The test runs `room empty`, one enemy at (9.5, 4), a player who stays still, `end 3`. It expects exactly one `a <kind> rears back` in 3 s.
- A standing player is lunged at again and again (criterion 4). The traces show the repeat directly:
  - runner at 30 FPS: second `a runner rears back` at 1.167 s;
  - runner at 60 FPS: second `a runner rears back` at 1.100 s.
- For the grub, the count of 3 matches the derived wind-up times of about 0.03 s, 1.47 s and 2.8 s (derived: recovery 0.8 s after each lunge, with the hurt window in between). The brute's second wind-up is derived at about 2.1 s, so the same test would fail for it too.
- V-a says each wind-up prints its message once. It does not say "one wind-up per 3 s". My reading is that the test expectation is wrong, not the build. The test stops at its first failure (grub, 30 FPS). Runner, brute and 60 FPS are therefore not yet evaluated by this test, and the same wrong expectation will fail them as well.
- Not a spec question. Route to Build (test tooling). The test must count one message per wind-up (a 1.2 s window or counting wind-up events is enough). QA did not change the test.
