### 1. `run_tests` (whole suite)

Ran 64 tests. 63 passed, 1 failed:

- `VariantMessageTest.test_wind_up_and_hit_messages_name_the_variant`
- Failure: `AssertionError: 3 != 1 : (30, 'grub')` at `out.count("a grub rears back") == 1`.
- Script: `room empty`, `fps 30`, `spawn grub 9.5 4`, `end 3`.
- Cause (derived from my traces, not from the code): in 3 s a grub winds up repeatedly. The runner trace shows a second `a runner rears back` at 1.167 s within 1.2 s. For the grub, wind-up (0.5 s), lunge, recovery (0.8 s) and approach fit about three wind-ups in 3 s. Each wind-up prints its message once, which is what V-a requires. The test counts all wind-ups in the session, so it expects 1 where 3 is correct behaviour.
- The assertion aborted that test, so the runner and brute cases and the 60 FPS cases of this test never ran. They are covered only by my traces below.
- Expected behaviour is stated in V-a ("each wind-up prints its message once"). The suite must pass, so this is a failed run. It goes to Build.

The other tests passed, including these:
- `test_rooms_match_the_spec`, which includes room 3 as 3 spitters plus grub (15,0).
- `test_room_3_clears_only_when_spitters_and_grub_are_all_dead` (R3-7, both rates).
