## Defect and questions

1. **Defect (manual, `repo/manual.md` as supplied).** In the Warnings list, the line after the "Being hit shows…" bullet begins with a leftover marker, `>>>REPLACE_PLACEHOLDER A swipe that interrupts the warning of a grub or runner staggers it for 0.4 s; a brute is not interrupted.` This is player-visible junk text. The stagger sentence is also now detached from the grub bullet. I saw this only in the packet copy; I did not run anything against it. Severity: low to medium (wording only). The fix is in Build: remove the marker and put the sentence back in a sensible place. The two new manual sentences are present and match the spec.
2. **Spec inconsistency in W1.** The bullet for spitter (15,7) reads `from the east` and then says south-east is expected. The formula (ratio 3/7 = 0.43 > 0.4142) gives `south-east`, and the build prints that at both rates. I treated the formula as governing. The designer should correct the bullet.
3. **W3 question.** Is a reaction rule meant to act on a hit message shown during hit-stun? If the stun discards that input, the `by a shot` script is not a valid test. The rule needs a trigger that is not swallowed, or a baseline to compare against.
