# Enemy-side combat depth (G-002, Option A) — design spec

Extends `design/combat.md` (build 0.3). From the build that implements this spec, the **Enemies** and
**Rooms** sections of `combat.md` are replaced by the Grub/Spitter/Charger/Shieldbearer definitions
and the room table below. Player kit (attack, dash, getting hit) is unchanged.

Authority: D-001 (one attack button plus dash), D-002 (fix monotony through enemy behavior and room
composition; no new input).

> Terminology: the Charger's attack is called a **rush** in this document, on purpose. Nothing here
> involves holding, charging or any new player action. Hold-to-charge is out of scope (D-002).

## 1. Experience intent

Across the 5 rooms the player must keep making different decisions: where to stand, when to dash,
and which enemy to deal with first. Corner-and-swipe must stop being a universal answer. Every
threat is shown before it can hurt (pillar 3), and the answer to every threat uses only attack and
dash (pillar 1).

## 2. Global rules

### 2.1 Time and frame rate

- All durations, speeds and cooldowns are in **seconds** and **tiles per second**, advanced by
  elapsed time, never by frame count.
- At 30 and 60 FPS the same inputs at the same times must give the same outcome, within a tolerance
  of one 30 FPS frame (about 0.033 s).
- Any fast movement (the rush, projectiles) must not skip over the player between frames. Contact is
  checked along the path travelled in the frame, not only at the end position.

### 2.2 Telegraph rules (apply to every damaging enemy action except Grub contact)

- **T1** Every enemy action that can damage the player has a **telegraph phase** (visible wind-up),
  then an **active phase** (can damage), then a **recovery phase**. Damage only occurs in the active
  phase.
- **T2** At the start of the telegraph a **danger marker** appears on the floor, covering the area
  the active phase will harm (to within a quarter tile), and the enemy visibly changes to a
  wind-up pose or tint. Both use the saturated orange/red threat language (art direction).
- **T3** Telegraph durations are never shorter than 0.5 s. Values are in the enemy tables.
- **T4** Direction and area are **locked** when the telegraph starts. They do not track the player
  afterwards. This is what makes the threat dodgeable and readable.
- **T5** The marker disappears when the active phase ends. If the enemy dies, the whole action is
  cancelled at once, marker included.
- **T6** Player attacks do not interrupt or stagger a telegraphing enemy (only death cancels).
  No new stagger system is introduced.
- **T7** No enemy begins a telegraph within **1.0 s** of the room starting, so the player can
  orient first.
- **T8** Damage reuses the existing getting-hit rule (`combat.md`): 1 heart, 0.3 s hit-stun, then
  invulnerability until 0.8 s after the hit. No new damage values or rules exist. If several active
  phases reach the player together, only one heart is lost, because of that invulnerability.

### 2.3 Dash interaction (reuses the existing dash rule)

- The player is invulnerable for the full 0.25 s dash. An active phase that overlaps the player
  during the dash does nothing to the player and is **not** used up: it keeps going. If it still
  overlaps the player on the first moment they are vulnerable again, the normal hit rule applies.
- Dash cooldown is 0.6 s. Every telegraph that is meant to be answered by dash lasts at least 0.6 s
  (Spitter 0.6, Charger 0.7, Shieldbearer bash 0.7), so a player who dashed just before a telegraph
  started has the dash ready by the time the active phase begins.
- The dash-related defect in `sim/build-0.3.md` (damage partway through a dash right after being
  hit) belongs to G-001. This spec assumes the dash rule in `combat.md` holds as written.

## 3. Enemies

### 3.1 Grub (unchanged)

HP 2. Walks straight at the player; bites on contact for 1 damage. No telegraph: its approach is
the visible threat. Reuses the existing contact rule. Its role is pressure that forces decisions
about the other enemies.

### 3.2 Spitter (telegraph added)

HP 2. Stationary. One shot cycle every **2.0 s**, measured from one telegraph start to the next.

| Phase | Duration | What the player sees |
|---|---|---|
| Telegraph | 0.6 s | Muzzle glows orange/red; a thin aim line from the Spitter to the first wall or the player, locked at telegraph start |
| Active | on release | Existing slow projectile along the locked line, 1 damage on hit |
| Idle | rest of the 2.0 s cycle | Normal look |

- Projectile speed, size and damage are as in build 0.3.
- Projectiles pass over other enemies and do not hurt them (assumed to match build 0.3; see the
  handoff's open points for the Technical Director).

### 3.3 Charger (new)

HP 3. Its job is to make the player leave the corner, or dash.

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Approach speed | 1.5 tiles/s (walks toward the player; no damage while walking) |
| Starts telegraph when | player is within 6 tiles and the Charger has not rushed in the last 1.5 s |
| Telegraph | 0.7 s, rush lane locked at the start |
| Rush lane | 1.5 tiles wide, up to 8 tiles long, straight from the Charger in the locked direction |
| Rush speed | 8 tiles/s |
| Rush damage | 1 (existing hit rule), only while rushing |
| Recovery after hitting a wall or another enemy | 1.2 s |
| Recovery after hitting the player, or after the full 8 tiles | 0.6 s |

Rules:

- **Telegraph:** the Charger stops, lowers its head and glows; the whole rush lane is drawn as an
  orange/red marker on the floor (T2, T4).
- **Rush:** moves along the lane at constant speed. It ends at the first of: a wall, another enemy
  (treated like a wall), the player (after dealing damage), or 8 tiles travelled.
- **Recovery:** the Charger stands still, visibly dazed, and cannot damage. This is the player's
  opening: with HP 3 and the 0.35 s attack recovery, a 1.2 s recovery is enough for a three-swipe
  kill if the player starts at once.
- A rush that misses the player into open floor leaves a shorter 0.6 s opening, so punishing it is
  possible but tighter.
- After recovery the Charger returns to approaching.
- **Answers using only walk, attack and dash:** (a) leave the lane before the rush starts; (b)
  dash so that the dash covers the moment the Charger reaches the player (the invulnerable window
  is 0.25 s); (c) punish the recovery. Standing in a corner with the lane pointing at you leaves
  (b) as the main answer.

### 3.4 Shieldbearer (new)

HP 3. Its job is to make "face it and swipe" fail, and to reward getting around it or baiting it.

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Walk speed | 1.0 tile/s toward the player |
| Turn rate (when not telegraphing or recovering) | 90°/s toward the player |
| Guard arc | 120° total (±60° from its facing), measured from the Shieldbearer to the attacking player |
| Bash starts telegraph when | player is within 1.5 tiles and inside the guard arc, and 1.0 s have passed since the last bash recovery |
| Bash telegraph | 0.7 s, facing and zone locked |
| Bash zone | front arc of 120°, 1.5 tiles long |
| Bash active | 0.15 s, 1 damage (existing hit rule) |
| Bash recovery | 1.0 s with the **guard down** |

Rules:

- **Guarded:** a player attack that lands from inside the guard arc deals **0** damage and gives
  distinct feedback (a visible spark on the shield). It does not push anyone back and does not
  stagger.
- **Unguarded:** a player attack that lands from outside the arc (sides or back) deals normal damage.
  During bash telegraph, active and recovery phases the Shieldbearer does not turn, and during
  bash recovery the **guard is down**, so front attacks also deal damage. The guard-down state must
  be visibly different (shield lowered, orange/red tint on the body).
- **Getting past the guard, using only attack and dash:**
  1. **Flank:** dash to its side or behind it, then swipe before it turns. At 90°/s, a player
     directly behind needs 120° of turning (about 1.3 s) before it can guard again, which allows a
     full three-swipe kill if the swipes begin at once. A player at its side has a shorter window.
  2. **Bait:** stand inside the arc until the bash telegraph starts, dash out of the zone (or
     through the active moment), then swipe the front during guard-down recovery.
- Grubs, Spitter projectiles and the Charger's rush are not affected by the shield.
- Shield and bash use only the existing hit and invulnerability rules.

## 4. Rooms

All enemies are present at room start. T7 applies. A room ends when all enemies are defeated
(unchanged). Room sizes and layouts are as in build 0.3. Compositions only; no layout work is
requested here.

| Room | Enemies | The question the room asks | Why it differs |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1 Charger, 2 Grubs | When do I dash or leave the lane? | Introduces telegraph and rush. A single Charger, with Grubs forcing the player out of a corner |
| 2 | 1 Shieldbearer, 2 Grubs | How do I get around the guard while Grubs press me? | Introduces guard. Which to handle first (Grubs or the shield) |
| 3 | 2 Spitters, 1 Charger | Where do I stand so two aim lines and one rush lane do not overlap me? | Positioning among several locked lanes; first room where lanes cross |
| 4 | 2 Shieldbearers, 1 Spitter, 1 Grub | Which target first, and how do I use the bash recovery? | Two guards, so flank and bait must be chosen per target. The Spitter keeps fire coming while you work |
| 5 | 1 Charger, 1 Shieldbearer, 1 Spitter, 2 Grubs (5 total) | Everything together: priority, position, dash timing | Final exam: uses every enemy; no single answer covers it |

Room size stays within the existing 3–6 enemies. Total enemy HP is higher than in build 0.3, so
run length needs checking (see acceptance criteria).

## 5. Edge cases

| Case | Rule |
|---|---|
| Player kills an enemy mid-telegraph | Action and marker cancelled immediately (T5) |
| Player is hit-stunned (0.3 s) or hit-invulnerable when an active phase arrives | Normal hit rules apply; telegraph and active phase are unchanged and the marker is still shown |
| Charger rush overlaps the player during a dash | No damage, rush continues; if still overlapping when the dash ends, normal hit applies (2.3) |
| Charger rushes into a Grub or Shieldbearer | Treated like a wall: rush ends, 1.2 s recovery. No damage to the other enemy |
| Two attack active phases hit together | One heart lost (T8) |
| Player attack queued during a dash lands on a Shieldbearer's guard | Same rules as any attack; arc decided at the moment of impact |
| Player attacks a Shieldbearer exactly at an arc edge | The 120° arc is inclusive of its boundary on the guarded side; ties resolve in favour of guarded |
| Charger spawns with the player already within 6 tiles | T7 still applies: no telegraph for 1.0 s |
| Shieldbearer cannot turn because it is in telegraph or recovery | Facing locked, as specified |
| Frame rate 30 vs 60 | Section 2.1 |
| Player holds the attack button | Nothing new happens (D-001, D-002) |

## 6. Acceptance criteria

### 6.1 Verification checks (QA, observable)

- **V1** In every room, before any enemy attack other than Grub contact can hurt the player, an
  orange/red marker was visible for at least 0.5 s. Checked at both 30 and 60 FPS.
- **V2** The marker's shape matches the area that actually hurts: standing outside it when the
  action resolves causes no damage; standing inside it, without being invulnerable, causes exactly 1
  heart.
- **V3** A dash that covers the moment the action reaches the player avoids all damage; a dash that
  ends before, or starts after, does not. The same timing gives the same result at 30 and 60 FPS
  (within one 30 FPS frame).
- **V4** The Charger's lane stays where it was when the telegraph began, even if the player moves;
  the Charger stops at a wall and is then visibly dazed and harmless; the player can attack it
  freely during that time.
- **V5** Swiping a Shieldbearer from the front deals no damage and shows distinct feedback; swiping
  from the side or back damages it; swiping the front during bash recovery damages it.
- **V6** A tester using only walk, attack and dash can defeat a Shieldbearer by flanking, and
  separately by baiting its bash.
- **V7** Killing an enemy during its telegraph removes its marker and no damage follows.
- **V8** Each room contains exactly the enemies in the room table, and no enemy telegraphs in the
  first second of a room.
- **V9** No new button does anything; holding attack changes nothing.
- **V10** Two simultaneous hits cost one heart, not two.

### 6.2 Experience signals (playtest and Review; keep away from the Playtester's handoff)

- A player who just holds a corner and swipes can no longer clear rooms 1–5 without clearly
  losing hearts. The old tactic stops working as a general answer.
- The dash is used in combat in every room, on purpose, to avoid or get past something, not just to
  cross the room.
- Without being told, a player can say how each enemy type must be fought differently (Grub:
  kill before contact; Spitter: avoid lanes; Charger: leave the lane or dash; Shieldbearer: get
  around or bait).
- Players describe the five rooms as asking different things (for example "room 2 was about the
  shield, room 3 was about where to stand"), and attention does not drop in rooms 4–5.
- When a player is hit, they can say what they should have seen or done; no hit feels unseen
  (pillar 3).
- A full run still lands within the 15–20 minute target (pillar 2).
- The "want a heavy hit" remark is not addressed here; if it recurs after this build, that is
  evidence for the D-002 revisit conditions.

## 7. Open questions and follow-ups

For `art-director` (visual spec requested; this spec does not decide them):

1. Danger marker look: shape language, fill versus outline, pulse or fill-up to show time left,
   exact orange/red values, and readability on the lantern-lit cave floor. Needs one language for
   Spitter aim line, Charger lane and Shieldbearer bash arc.
2. Enemy wind-up poses and tints for Spitter, Charger and Shieldbearer bash.
3. Shieldbearer's guard presentation: how the front reads at a glance, the blocked-hit spark, and
   the guard-down state.
4. Charger's dazed recovery pose.
5. Whether marker readability needs a non-colour cue (for example shape or pulse) in addition to
   orange/red.

For `technical-director` (checks or assumptions, not decided here): see the outgoing handoff.

No conflict with D-001 or pillar 3 was found.

# 06: game-designer → technical-director — plan the build of Option A enemy-side combat depth

## Task

Make a technical plan for building the spec in `design/enemy-depth.md`: telegraphed attacks, the
Charger, the Shieldbearer, and new compositions for the 5 existing rooms. Split the work into
buildable, verifiable parts and call out risks.

## Context

Goal G-002: the Owner said 「戦闘が単調なので改善したい」. The playtest (01–02) found monotony. The
diagnosis (03) offered options. The Owner chose Option A on 2026-10-10 (D-002): fix it through enemy
behavior and room composition, keep one-button combat. This handoff delivers the design spec.

## Findings

- The spec is in **`design/enemy-depth.md`** (new file). `design/combat.md` is unchanged; the spec
  says it supersedes combat.md's Enemies and Rooms sections from the implementing build.
- Summary of the spec:
  - **Telegraph rules (section 2.2):** every damaging enemy action except Grub contact has telegraph
    → active → recovery phases. A floor danger marker in orange/red appears for at least 0.5 s,
    direction/area locked at telegraph start, no damage before the active phase, no stagger, death
    cancels everything.
  - **Dash (section 2.3):** reuses the existing full-duration dash invulnerability; an active phase
    that overlaps a dashing player is not used up, so it can still hurt if it overlaps when the dash
    ends. Telegraphs that expect a dash are at least as long as the 0.6 s dash cooldown.
  - **Spitter:** telegraph 0.6 s, cycle 2.0 s, locked aim line. **Grub:** unchanged.
  - **Charger:** HP 3; telegraph 0.7 s with a 1.5-tile-wide, up-to-8-tile lane; rush 8 tiles/s;
    recovery 1.2 s after a wall or enemy, 0.6 s after a player hit or a full-length miss.
  - **Shieldbearer:** HP 3; 120° guard arc, front hits deal 0 with feedback; 90°/s turn rate; bash
    with 0.7 s telegraph, then 1.0 s guard-down recovery. Player gets past it by flanking after a
    dash, or by baiting the bash.
  - **Rooms:** R1 Charger + 2 Grubs; R2 Shieldbearer + 2 Grubs; R3 2 Spitters + Charger; R4 2
    Shieldbearers + Spitter + Grub; R5 Charger + Shieldbearer + Spitter + 2 Grubs.
  - **Acceptance criteria** are in section 6 (V1–V10 for QA, experience signals separately).
- All timings are in seconds and tiles per second. At 30 and 60 FPS outcomes must match within one
  30 FPS frame; fast movement needs path-based contact checks, not end-position checks.
- Art follow-ups for `art-director` are listed in spec section 7 (marker look, wind-up poses, guard
  presentation, dazed pose, non-colour cue). They are not decided in this spec.

## Decisions already made

- D-001: one attack button plus dash, unchanged.
- D-002: enemy behavior and room composition only; no heavy attack, no new input, **no
  hold-to-charge** in this Goal. (The spec calls the Charger's attack a "rush" to avoid confusion.)

## Constraints

- No new input buttons. Holding attack must do nothing new.
- Must play correctly at 30 and 60 FPS.
- Small team: reuse existing systems (hit, invulnerability, dash, contact rules, enemy controller).
- Stay inside the 5-room cave slice. No layout, biome, meta-progression or run-length changes.
- Pillar 3: every threat is visible before it can hurt.

## Open questions

For the Technical Director to confirm or answer in the plan. None of them changes the design
direction; if one does, hand back with a question.

1. Does the current dash pass through enemies, or stop against them? The spec's flank and bait
   routes should work with sideways dashes, but a dash through the Shieldbearer's space would be
   an additional route. Please report the actual behavior.
2. Player walk speed is not in `combat.md`. The spec assumes the player is clearly faster than the
   Shieldbearer (1.0 tile/s) and the Charger's approach (1.5 tiles/s). Please confirm or report the
   value.
3. Enemy-versus-enemy collision: the spec assumes a Charger's rush stops at any enemy as at a
   wall, and that Spitter projectiles pass over enemies without hurting them. Please confirm
   whether this matches existing behavior; if not, say what is cheapest.
4. Cost and approach for generalising telegraph → active → recovery across enemies, and for the
   "hit check along the path" requirement at 30 FPS.
5. Whether total enemy HP in rooms 4–5 risks the 15–20 minute run target; flag if a measurement is
   needed in the verification plan.
6. The dash defect in G-001 should be fixed or confirmed fixed before V3 can be verified reliably;
   please order the work accordingly.

## Expected output

A technical plan (Skill `technical-plan`): approach, work breakdown, order, risks, and how each
spec check V1–V10 will be verified, including at 30 and 60 FPS. It goes next to implementation. It
also lists art-director's visual spec (spec section 7) as a dependency or parallel task.
